HISTORIC WASHINGTON SQUARE • CIVIC HEART

Where Tradition, Commerce, and Safety Meet

From Good Neighbor Days festivals to summer concerts at the pavilion and community gatherings, Washington Police officers are dedicated to serving residents, businesses, and visitors across Tazewell County.

HISTORIC BADGE HERITAGE

The Fountain at the Center of Our Badge

Centennial civic symbol: The central fountain at Washington Square is featured directly on the official department badge and seal. It stands at the center of Washington's historic square, community events, and downtown commerce.

24/7 MARKED PATROL FLEET

High-Visibility Neighborhood Patrol

Marked cruiser fleet: Marked Ford Interceptor utility vehicles serve across all three municipal sectors. Outfitted with mobile data terminals, dash cams, and medical AED units, officers respond to traffic safety needs and emergency calls throughout Washington.

CAMPUS SAFETY • D.A.R.E. CADRE

School Resource & Youth Safety

Active educational partnership: Dedicated School Resource Officers maintain daily safety partnerships across Central Primary, District 51, and Washington Community High School. Officers teach the D.A.R.E. curriculum, provide campus security, and mentor local students.

CITIZEN PARTICIPATION & EDUCATION

Community Outreach Programs

Explore how Washington residents can actively collaborate with officers through classes, neighborhood safety networks, and youth educational initiatives.

CITIZEN ACADEMY • 10-WEEK PROGRAM

Citizen Police Academy

Hands-on community course: Open to Washington residents aged 18 and older. Participants experience patrol ride-alongs, firearms safety simulations, crime scene processing, radar speed enforcement, and judicial procedures.

Download Application (PDF) ↗
YOUTH SAFETY • 5TH GRADE CURRICULUM

D.A.R.E. Substance Prevention

Evidence-based decision-making: Certified Washington police officers instruct the 10-week D.A.R.E. curriculum across all District 50, 51, and 52 elementary schools, equipping students with critical thinking, refusal skills, and conflict resolution tactics.

Active in All Elementary Schools
CAMPUS PRESENCE • DISTRICT PARTNERSHIP

School Resource Officers (SRO)

Daily campus protection: Full-time sworn officers stationed at Washington Community High School and Central Middle School provide security, de-escalation, informal counseling, and law-related classroom education.

WCHS & Central Middle School
RESIDENTIAL NETWORK • WATCH BLOCK

Neighborhood & Business Watch

Proactive neighborhood vigilance: Neighbors collaborate with patrol sector supervisors to identify suspicious activity, review security trends, and coordinate block-by-block emergency preparedness.

TRAUMA MITIGATION • CRISIS COMFORT

Stuffed Animal Comfort Program

Child emotional support: Every marked squad car carries brand-new plush stuffed animals provided by community donors to comfort children experiencing domestic calls, vehicle accidents, or medical emergencies.

Standard Squad Car Equipment
CRISIS AID • MUNICIPAL VOUCHERS

Salvation Army Service Unit

Immediate compassionate assistance: Administered in partnership with the local Salvation Army Service Unit, officers provide emergency vouchers for temporary emergency shelter, gasoline, food, and urgent necessities to families facing crisis.
