District 1 • Historic Core & East (434 Acres)

District 1: Historic Core & East Sector Patrol

Downtown Historic Square, Devonshire & North Main Campus: Encompasses Washington's historic Downtown Square, municipal complex, WPD Police Headquarters, Devonshire Estates, Washington Middle School (District 52 on North Main), Lincoln Grade School, and the Washington Community High School (District 308) campus. Covers downtown commercial properties, school traffic zones, and surrounding residential neighborhoods.

  • Historic Downtown Square, City Hall & WPD HQ (115 W Jefferson)
  • Devonshire Estates & Devonshire Road Residential Corridor
  • Washington Middle School (District 52 on North Main) & WCHS

District 2 • West Sector (1,681 Acres)

District 2: West Sector & Freedom Parkway Hub

Freedom Parkway Retail Hub & Peoria Street Corridor: Encompasses western Washington from South Cummings Lane and Centennial Drive west to municipal boundaries, including the Freedom Parkway retail center (Walmart Supercenter), ALDI, and Meadow Valley Park. Focuses on retail loss deterrence, Peoria Street traffic calming, and western residential neighborhood presence.

  • Freedom Parkway Retail Center & Walmart Supercenter
  • ALDI Commercial Corridor (1110 Peoria St / Bus 24)
  • Meadow Valley Park & Western Residential Neighborhoods
Sector Unit Sector Car 102
Supervision West Sector Sgt
Municipal Area 1,681 Acres
District 3 • North Sector (1,477 Acres)

District 3: North Sector & Route 24 Bypass

Five Points, Central School Campus & Bypass Corridor: Encompasses northern Washington along the US Route 24 Bypass, Five Points Washington on North Wilmor, Central Primary School (1400 Newcastle Rd), Central Intermediate School (1301 Eagle Ave), Cherry Tree Shopping Center, and West Cruger Road. Operations prioritize school arrival safety, community center security, and highway corridor traffic management.

  • Five Points Washington & Fire Station 1 (N Wilmor Rd)
  • Central Primary & Central Intermediate Schools (District 51)
  • US Route 24 Bypass Corridor & Cherry Tree Center
