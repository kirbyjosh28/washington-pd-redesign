What Do You Need Help With?

Direct pathways to the four most frequent resident situations in Washington. Clear rules, official legal authority, and immediate action without intermediate pages.

OVERNIGHT PARKING

Need to park on the street overnight?

On-street parking is prohibited between 2:00 AM and 6:00 AM. Eligible residents may request up to 3 consecutive nights of temporary exemption before 2:00 AM. Winter snow restrictions apply if snowfall exceeds 2 inches.

AUTHORITY: Washington City Code § 72.02
CITATIONS & FINES

Need to pay a ticket or city ordinance fine?

Settle parking tickets and city ordinance violations 24/7 via Tyler Technologies or automated phone pay at (877) 813-6421. Fines must be satisfied or contested within 14 days to avoid statutory late penalties.

AUTHORITY: City Financial Services • Tyler Portal

FREE COMMUNITY DROP-OFF

Safe Medication Disposal

A permanent, secure prescription drug disposal kiosk is located in the lobby of the Washington Police Department at 115 W. Jefferson St. This service is free, anonymous, and requires no paperwork or registration.
Accepted Items:
  • Prescription medications (Schedule II–V controlled and non-controlled substances)
  • Over-the-counter pills, capsules, tablets, and patches
  • Vitamins, nutritional supplements, and pet medications
Items NOT Accepted:
  • Needles, syringes, EpiPens, and sharps (dispose via Tazewell County Health Dept)
  • Inhalers, aerosol spray cans, and mercury thermometers
  • Hydrogen peroxide, lotions, and bulk liquid containers
Headquarters Lobby Access: Open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM. For questions or assistance, contact non-emergency dispatch at (309) 444-2313.
Call Dispatch (309) 444-2313
STATUTORY PUBLIC RECORDS

Police Records & FOIA

Submit Directly via City FOIA Portal ↗
Filing Options: Records requests under 5 ILCS 140 may be submitted directly online above, at the headquarters records window (Mon–Fri 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM), by fax to (309) 444-7511, or generated using this offline form.

Under the Illinois Freedom of Information Act, public records will be provided within 5 business days (or 21 days for commercial requests).

100% CONFIDENTIAL

Anonymous Crime Tip

Emergency Notice: For active crimes or immediate threats, dial 911. Anonymous tips may also be phoned to Greater Peoria Crime Stoppers at (309) 673-9000 or WPD dispatch at (309) 444-2313.

No personal identifying information is collected. Your tip is reviewed by the Washington Police Investigations Division.

FREE APPOINTMENT

Child Safety Seat Check

Appointment Scheduling: Free inspections are conducted by certified technicians at WPD headquarters (115 W. Jefferson St). To schedule directly by phone, call (309) 444-2313.

Certified child passenger safety technicians inspect car seats, check for manufacturer recalls, and demonstrate proper vehicle installation.
