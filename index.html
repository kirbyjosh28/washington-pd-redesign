OPERATIONAL COVERAGE & READINESS

Patrol Operations & Shift Schedule

Washington Police operates continuous 24/7/365 active emergency coverage through three dedicated shifts, supported by bi-annual seniority bidding and round-the-clock supervisory oversight.

SUPERVISORY PATROL STRUCTURE

Patrol Shift Schedule & Coverage

Continuous 24/7/365 field presence: Three dedicated shifts ensure that certified officers and field supervisors are always on active patrol across all Washington sectors.

Day Shift 07:00 – 15:00
Focus: School arrival zones, downtown business district, municipal court appearances, and morning commuter corridors.
Afternoon Shift 15:00 – 23:00
Focus: School dismissal crossings, evening retail corridors, park patrols, high school athletic events, and peak traffic management.
Night Shift 23:00 – 07:00
Focus: Commercial business security door checks, overnight residential patrols, targeted radar speed enforcement, and vacation house checks.
* Shift rotations and regular days off are bid by department seniority twice annually (November and May) under the PBLC Collective Bargaining Agreement.

FREE COMMUNITY DROP-OFF

Safe Medication Disposal

A permanent, secure prescription drug disposal kiosk is located in the lobby of the Washington Police Department at 115 W. Jefferson St. This service is free, anonymous, and requires no paperwork or registration.
Accepted Items:
  • Prescription medications (Schedule II–V controlled and non-controlled substances)
  • Over-the-counter pills, capsules, tablets, and patches
  • Vitamins, nutritional supplements, and pet medications
Items NOT Accepted:
  • Needles, syringes, EpiPens, and sharps (dispose via Tazewell County Health Dept)
  • Inhalers, aerosol spray cans, and mercury thermometers
  • Hydrogen peroxide, lotions, and bulk liquid containers
Headquarters Lobby Access: Open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM. For questions or assistance, contact non-emergency dispatch at (309) 444-2313.
