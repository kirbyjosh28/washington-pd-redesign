Accountability and Leadership

Guiding our sworn and civilian personnel with experienced leadership, personal accountability, community alignment, and independent statutory civilian oversight.

HEADQUARTERS • 115 W. JEFFERSON ST
EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP • 25+ YEARS OF WPD SERVICE

Chief Jeff Stevens

Department leadership: A veteran of the Washington Police Department with more than 25 years of dedicated law enforcement service, Chief Jeff Stevens was appointed Chief of Police in June 2025. He previously served as Deputy Chief and is a proud graduate of the prestigious Northwestern University School of Police Staff and Command (2006) as well as an honorable veteran of the Illinois Air National Guard.

“Our mission is to serve the citizens of Washington with fairness, transparency, and professional excellence in every call for service.”
Direct Line: (309) 444-2313
CIVILIAN & MAYORAL PARTNERSHIP

City Council & Mayoral Partnership

Municipal alignment: Chief Stevens collaborates closely with Mayor Lilija Stevens and the Washington City Council to ensure policy transparency, departmental budgeting, and public safety priorities that mirror community values.

Bi-monthly Public Council Meetings • City Hall Chambers
INDEPENDENT STATUTORY OVERSIGHT

Board of Police Commissioners

Statutory civilian governance: The City of Washington Board of Police Commissioners is an independent, civilian-appointed body responsible for conducting competitive entry examinations, promotional testing, and independent grievance oversight.
