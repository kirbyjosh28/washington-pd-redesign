Accountability and Leadership

Guiding our sworn and civilian personnel with experienced leadership, personal accountability, community alignment, and independent statutory civilian oversight.

Executive Command: Chief Jeff Stevens • 2 Deputy Chiefs
Supervisory Cadre: 24/7 Shift Sergeants
Civilian Oversight: Board of Police Commissioners
HEADQUARTERS • 115 W. JEFFERSON ST
EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP • 25+ YEARS OF WPD SERVICE

Chief Jeff Stevens

Department leadership: With more than 25 years of service in the Washington Police Department, Chief Jeff Stevens was appointed Chief of Police in June 2025 after ten years as Deputy Chief. He graduated from the Northwestern University School of Police Staff and Command in 2006 and served in the Illinois Air National Guard.

“Our mission is to serve the citizens of Washington with fairness, transparency, and professional excellence in every call for service.”
Direct Line: (309) 444-2313
CIVILIAN & MAYORAL PARTNERSHIP

City Council & Mayoral Partnership

Municipal alignment: Chief Stevens collaborates closely with Mayor Lilija Stevens and the Washington City Council to ensure policy transparency, departmental budgeting, and public safety priorities that mirror community values.

Bi-monthly Public Council Meetings • City Hall Chambers
INDEPENDENT STATUTORY OVERSIGHT

Board of Police Commissioners

Statutory civilian governance: The City of Washington Board of Police Commissioners is an independent, civilian-appointed body responsible for conducting competitive entry examinations, promotional testing, and independent grievance oversight.

Operational & Administrative Command

Sworn in March 2026 following Washington City Council authorization, our two Deputy Chiefs oversee frontline field operations, criminal investigations, recruit standards, and administrative transparency under Chief Stevens.

EXECUTIVE COMMAND • FIELD OPERATIONS & STANDARDS
18+ Yrs WPD Sworn March 2026

Deputy Chief Steve Smith

Deputy Chief of Operations

Operational leadership: An 18-year veteran of the Washington Police Department, Deputy Chief Steve Smith was sworn in as Deputy Chief of Operations in March 2026. Joining WPD in 2008, he was named Officer of the Year in 2011 and previously served as Detective Sergeant. A federal Task Force Officer with Homeland Security Investigations (HSI) since 2016, he specializes in digital forensics, crime scene reconstruction, and investigations with the Illinois Internet Crimes Against Children (ICAC) task force. Deputy Chief Smith is an alumnus of Anderson University.

Core Command Responsibilities
24/7 Patrol Watch Supervision CIERT SWAT Tactical Readiness HSI & ICAC Task Forces Digital Forensics & Evidence
Dispatch Line: (309) 444-2313
EXECUTIVE COMMAND • ADMINISTRATION & INNOVATION
13+ Yrs WPD Sworn March 2026

Deputy Chief Brian Simpson

Deputy Chief of Administration

Administrative stewardship: A 13-year veteran of the Washington Police Department, Deputy Chief Brian Simpson joined the agency in 2013, earned Officer of the Year honors in 2018, and was promoted to Sergeant in 2019 before taking command as Deputy Chief of Administration in March 2026. A Trustee on the Washington Police Pension Board, he manages state training mandates and department technology systems, and began his public safety service as a youth explorer with the Mercer County Sheriff's Department.

Patrol & Investigative Sergeants

Field Sergeants are the direct supervisory backbone of the Washington Police Department, providing 24/7 shift incident command, active call triage, recruit field training, and continuous operational quality control.

SHIFT COMMAND • 24/7/365

Patrol Watch Sergeants

Watch 1 (Day) • Watch 2 (Evening) • Watch 3 (Midnight)

First-line field command: Watch Sergeants direct patrol shifts on Washington roadways, manage emergency call priorities, conduct roll-call briefings, and review officer field reports prior to supervisory approval.

  • Incident Command: Immediate on-scene coordination for critical emergency dispatches.
  • Daily Roll Call: Shift intelligence dissemination, tactical briefings, and equipment inspection.
  • Report Audits: First-line review and legal validation of all daily incident and crash filings.
Active 24/7/365 Shift Supervision
CRIMINAL INVESTIGATIONS • FORENSICS

Detective Sergeant

Felony Case Management & Specialized Units

Specialized casework: Supervises felony case investigations, major crime scene processing, search warrant executions, and digital forensic extractions. Coordinates directly with the Tazewell County State's Attorney's Office and regional enforcement task forces.

  • Case Management: Direct oversight of felony investigations and forensic evidence handling.
  • Prosecutorial Liaison: Coordination of felony warrants and grand jury filings with Tazewell County.
  • Regional Task Forces: Active supervisory coordination with CIERT Tactical Team and ICAC.
Specialized Investigative Command
DEVELOPMENT & COMPLIANCE • ILETSB

Field Training Supervisors

FTO Program & Mandated Officer Standards

Professional excellence: Manages Washington's 14-week Field Training Officer (FTO) program for probationary recruits, enforces compliance with Illinois Law Enforcement Training and Standards Board (ILETSB) mandates, and oversees officer de-escalation certifications.

  • FTO Recruit Mentorship: Rigorous daily observation reports (DOR) and performance milestones.
  • Statutory Mandates: Annual SAFE-T Act and state law enforcement certification adherence.
  • Proficiency Standards: Defensive tactics, crisis de-escalation, and tactical firearms proficiency.
Standards & Training Compliance
STATUTORY QUALITY ASSURANCE & PUBLIC ACCOUNTABILITY

Mandatory 24-Hour Supervisory Review Process

Under Washington Police Department General Orders and Illinois statutory standards, every formal arrest, use-of-force incident, vehicle pursuit, and citizen inquiry is subjected to an independent first-line review by an on-duty Sergeant and the Division Deputy Chief within 24 hours of occurrence.
