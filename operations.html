CONTINUOUS 24/7/365 EMERGENCY COVERAGE

Patrol Operations & Shift Structure

The Washington Police Department maintains uninterrupted 24/7 emergency patrol coverage across all municipal sectors, governed by professional supervision, continuous state qualifications, and dedicated fleet technology.

3 Active Shifts: Day (07-15), Afternoon (15-23), Night (23-07)
Supervision: Dedicated Field Sergeants on Every Watch
Qualifications: 4x Annual Duty Weapon Certifications
SUPERVISORY PATROL STRUCTURE

Patrol Shift Schedule & Coverage

Continuous 24/7/365 field presence: Three dedicated shifts ensure that certified patrol officers and field supervisory sergeants are always on active duty across all Washington sectors.

Day Shift (Watch 1) 07:00 – 15:00
Operational Priorities: Elementary and high school arrival zones, downtown Washington Square commerce, Tazewell County court appearances, business sector checks, and morning commuter traffic safety.
Afternoon Shift (Watch 2) 15:00 – 23:00
Operational Priorities: School dismissal crossings, evening retail corridors, park and recreational trail patrols, Washington Community High School athletic events, and peak rush-hour incident management.
Night Shift (Watch 3) 23:00 – 07:00
Operational Priorities: Commercial business security door checks, overnight residential patrols, targeted radar speed enforcement along Route 24 and Peoria Hill, and registered vacation house checks.
* Shift assignments and regular days off are bid by department seniority twice annually (November and May) under the PBLC Collective Bargaining Agreement.
TACTICAL & CONSTITUTIONAL RIGOR

State-Certified Training & Equipment

Professional standard of care: The Washington Police Department enforces training, marksmanship, and constitutional standards that far exceed state minimum requirements.

Quarterly Duty Weapon Qualifications Sworn officers qualify with primary duty sidearms four times per calendar year, including low-light and stress firing scenarios.
Individually Issued Patrol Rifles Every patrol officer is individually issued a patrol rifle with optics, paired with mandatory annual tactical recertification.
State-Certified Master Range Instructors In-house range masters certified by the Illinois Law Enforcement Training and Standards Board (ILETSB).
Crisis De-Escalation & Mental Health Intervention Continuous certified training in crisis de-escalation, CIT (Crisis Intervention Team) protocols, and trauma-informed response.
Public Records & Administrative Window:
Monday – Friday 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM (115 W. Jefferson St)
Emergency Patrol & Dispatch: Continuous 24/7/365
MODERN FIELD EQUIPMENT & SAFETY

Patrol Fleet & In-Car Technology

Every marked cruiser is a mobile tactical workstation equipped with communications, trauma life support, and digital recording technology.

AWD PATROL FLEET

Ford Police Interceptors

All-weather pursuit-rated: Intelligent all-wheel drive, high-output powertrains, ballistic door panel protection, and custom low-profile Code 3 LED emergency warning lightbars for maximum intersection visibility.

REAL-TIME CAD LINK

Mobile Data Terminals

Toughbook workstations: Ruggedized vehicle laptops interfaced directly with Tazewell County TC3 dispatch, LEADS state warrant databases, and municipal GIS district mapping.

CIVILIAN & OFFICER SAFETY

Body & Fleet Cameras

Axon evidence capture: High-definition body-worn cameras and dual-angle squad dashcams with automatic holster activation and cloud-based chain of custody compliance.
