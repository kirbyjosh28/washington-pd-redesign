Police Officer Testing Steps

Conducted under the statutory jurisdiction of the City of Washington Board of Police Commissioners to ensure rigorous, merit-based selection.

STAGE 01

Application & Written Exam

Submit complete application package followed by the standardized written aptitude examination administered by the Board of Police Commissioners. Minimum 70% passing threshold required.

STAGE 02

POWER Fitness Test

Illinois Law Enforcement Training & Standards Board (ILETSB) Peace Officer Wellness Evaluation Report (POWER): sit & reach, 1-minute sit-ups, maximum bench press, and 1.5-mile run.

STAGE 03

Commission Oral Interview

Formal structured oral interview with the civilian Board of Police Commissioners evaluating communication skills, ethical decision-making, judgment, and municipal community fit.

STAGE 04

Deep Background & Polygraph

Thorough criminal history verification, credit analysis, neighbor/reference interviews, employment history vetting, and certified polygraph examination conducted by specialized investigators.

STAGE 05

Psychological, Medical & Academy

Psychological fitness evaluation, complete medical physical examination including drug screening, followed by appointment and enrollment in a certified Illinois Police Academy.
