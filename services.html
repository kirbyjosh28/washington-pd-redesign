FREE COMMUNITY DROP-OFF

Safe Medication Disposal

A permanent, secure prescription drug disposal kiosk is located in the lobby of the Washington Police Department at 115 W. Jefferson St. This service is free, anonymous, and requires no paperwork or registration.
Accepted Items:
  • Prescription medications (Schedule II–V controlled and non-controlled substances)
  • Over-the-counter pills, capsules, tablets, and patches
  • Vitamins, nutritional supplements, and pet medications
Items NOT Accepted:
  • Needles, syringes, EpiPens, and sharps (dispose via Tazewell County Health Dept)
  • Inhalers, aerosol spray cans, and mercury thermometers
  • Hydrogen peroxide, lotions, and bulk liquid containers
Call Dispatch (309) 444-2313
