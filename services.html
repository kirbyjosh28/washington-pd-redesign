Frequently Asked Questions & Situational Guidance

Authoritative, verified answers to common civic situations, ordinances, and procedures in the City of Washington. Every answer links directly to applicable municipal statutes and one-click action workflows.

Under Washington Municipal Code § 72.02, on-street parking is prohibited throughout the City of Washington between 2:00 AM and 6:00 AM without prior permission. Residents experiencing special circumstances (such as overnight house guests, driveway sealcoating/repairs, moving, or disabled vehicles) may request a temporary exemption for up to three (3) consecutive nights. Requests must be logged before 2:00 AM to prevent automatic citation.

Read Municipal Code § 72.02 ↗

Under Municipal Code § 72.03, whenever snowfall accumulates to two (2) inches or more, a citywide snow emergency is automatically in effect. Parking on any designated snow route or city street is strictly prohibited until the roadway has been plowed curb-to-curb by City Public Works crews. Overnight parking exemptions are immediately suspended during declared snow events, and vehicles obstructing snowplows are subject to immediate ticketing and towing at the owner's expense.

Read Snow Ordinance § 72.03 ↗ View District Cartography & Routes

Citations and ordinance fines can be paid 24/7 online via the Tyler Technologies Municipal Portal, by automated phone IVR at (877) 813-6421, or in person via the secure 24/7 lobby drop box at Police Headquarters (115 W. Jefferson St). Fines must be satisfied or formally contested within 14 calendar days of issuance to prevent statutory late penalties and administrative fees.

Pay Ticket on City Portal ↗ Tyler Technologies Portal ↗

Under Washington Municipal Code, impounded vehicles require payment of the $500 administrative tow fee prior to release. The registered owner must present: (1) valid driver's license or government photo identification, (2) current vehicle registration card, and (3) proof of active vehicle liability insurance. Administrative tow bonds may be paid online via the City payment portal or at the police counter (Mon–Fri 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM). Storage and hook fees are assessed separately by the private tow operator.

Pay Tow Bond Online ↗ Call Dispatch: (309) 444-2313

State of Illinois Motor Vehicle Crash Reports investigated by Washington Police officers are electronically uploaded to the BuyCrash portal (LexisNexis Risk Solutions). Reports are typically reviewed, approved, and available for download within 3 to 5 business days following the collision. To retrieve your report, you will need the date of the accident, the involved driver's last name, and either the WPD agency report number or driver's license number.

Search BuyCrash Portal ↗ Station Records: (309) 444-2313

Public records, dispatch audio, and non-exempt police incident documentation are provided under the Illinois Freedom of Information Act (5 ILCS 140/). By Illinois statute, the City of Washington must furnish a response within five (5) business days of receipt for non-commercial requests (21 business days for commercial requests). Requests can be submitted electronically using the City's online FOIA portal or filed in person at the records window.

Submit Police FOIA Online ↗ Call Records Window: (309) 444-1111

Under Chapter 16, Article III of the Washington Municipal Code, commercial solicitors must register with the City Clerk, undergo a criminal background screening, and obtain an official City Solicitor Permit. Permitted solicitors must visibly wear their City-issued photo identification badge at all times. Soliciting is permitted only between 9:00 AM and sunset (Monday–Saturday) and is strictly unlawful at any residence displaying a 'No Solicitors' notice. If an unpermitted solicitor approaches your home, contact non-emergency dispatch immediately.

Unused or expired solid prescription medications (tablets, capsules, patches) can be deposited 24/7 in the secure green drop box located in the Washington Police Headquarters lobby (115 W. Jefferson St) in partnership with the Tazewell County Health Department. Liquids, inhalers, and needles are prohibited. For residential medical sharps, syringes, and lancets, dispose of them safely in puncture-resistant rigid containers at Washington Fire Department Station 1 (200 N. Wilmor Rd) during staffed daytime hours.

When residents travel out of town for at least three (3) consecutive days, they may enroll their home in the Vacation House Watch program. On-duty patrol officers conduct periodic exterior physical checks of the premises, inspecting perimeter doors, ground-floor windows, gates, and exterior grounds for signs of tampering, vandalism, or storm damage, as call volume permits. You will be notified immediately if any irregularities or forced entry are discovered.

Every year, dozens of lost, abandoned, or stolen bicycles are recovered by Washington patrol officers and public works crews. Without a recorded serial number, establishing legal ownership is difficult. Voluntary registration records your bicycle's manufacturer, model, serial number, frame color, and distinguishing characteristics in the department's crime prevention index, allowing officers to instantly locate the registered owner upon recovery.
