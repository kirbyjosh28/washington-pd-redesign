Frequently Asked Questions & Service Navigator

Find quick answers to common questions about municipal ordinances, permits, dispatch procedures, and immediately initiate the corresponding digital service.

Parking & Traffic How do I request an overnight street parking waiver in Washington?
▼

City of Washington Ordinance prohibits on-street parking between 2:00 AM and 6:00 AM. If you have overnight guests, driveway paving, or an unexpected vehicle emergency, you can request temporary permission online or by contacting dispatch. Vehicles must still follow snow emergency declarations.

Ready to submit a parking exemption?
Parking & Traffic Where and how can I pay a parking ticket or municipal ordinance citation?
▼

Municipal ordinance citations and parking tickets can be paid online via our secure e-pay gateway, by mail with a check/money order, or in person at the City Hall City Collector window (301 Walnut St) during business hours. Payments made within 14 days avoid late penalty fees.

Community Safety How do I register my bicycle to protect against theft and help officers recover it?
▼

The Washington Police Department maintains a free municipal property recovery registry. Recording your bicycle's serial number, manufacturer, and frame description allows officers to instantly verify ownership when abandoned or stolen bicycles are recovered in city parks and neighborhoods.

Records & Reports How do I request official police records or submit an Illinois FOIA request?
▼

Public records requests are governed by the Illinois Freedom of Information Act (5 ILCS 140). Non-commercial requests receive a response within 5 business days. You can submit requests electronically via our secure portal, by email to the FOIA Officer, or in person at the 115 W. Jefferson St records desk.

Records & Reports How can I obtain a copy of an Illinois Motor Vehicle Crash Report?
▼

Washington Police crash reports are digitally transmitted to the State of Illinois repository. Certified crash reports can be searched, purchased, and downloaded 24/7 through the BuyCrash portal using your state agency case number, date of incident, or driver's last name.

Community Safety How does the Vacation House Watch program work while I am away?
▼

When you enroll, patrol officers on all three watches are notified of your absence, authorized keyholders, and emergency contact details. When emergency call volume allows, uniformed officers conduct periodic exterior inspections of doors, windows, and perimeter gates.

Enroll your residence for security checks:
Community Safety Where can I safely dispose of old medications or medical sharps needles?
▼

Unused prescription pills, capsules, and patches can be dropped into the secure 24/7 kiosk in the Washington Police Headquarters lobby at 115 W. Jefferson St. Medical sharps and needles are NOT accepted in the police lobby; they must be disposed of via the Washington Fire Department sharps program at Fire Station 1 (200 N. Wilmor Rd) in rigid, sealed containers.

View lobby drop box parameters:
Parking & Traffic How can I verify if a door-to-door solicitor is legally permitted in Washington?
▼

Any transient merchant or door-to-door solicitor must obtain a permit from the City Clerk following a WPD background check. Permitted solicitors must wear an official photo ID badge visible on their outer garment. If a solicitor refuses to show their permit or violates "No Soliciting" signs, notify dispatch immediately.

Check official municipal permitting registry: City Solicitor Registry ↗
Community Safety Is my identity truly protected when submitting an anonymous crime tip?
▼

Yes. The online tip submission form is encrypted and strictly strips all identifying metadata including IP addresses. You do not need to provide your name, phone number, or email address. Tips are routed directly to Criminal Investigations detectives for verification.

Provide confidential information to detectives:
Dispatch & 911 When should I dial 911 versus the 24/7 non-emergency dispatch line?
▼

Call 911 immediately for crimes in progress, physical violence, weapons, motor vehicle collisions with injuries, medical emergencies, or fires. For crimes not in progress (e.g., past theft, property damage, noise disputes, animal complaints) or to speak with an on-duty patrol officer, dial (309) 444-2313.

FREE COMMUNITY DROP-OFF

Safe Medication Disposal

A permanent, secure prescription drug disposal kiosk is located in the lobby of the Washington Police Department at 115 W. Jefferson St. This service is free, anonymous, and requires no paperwork or registration.
Accepted Items:
  • Prescription medications (Schedule II–V controlled and non-controlled substances)
  • Over-the-counter pills, capsules, tablets, and patches
  • Vitamins, nutritional supplements, and pet medications
Items NOT Accepted:
  • Needles, syringes, EpiPens, and sharps (dispose via Tazewell County Health Dept)
  • Inhalers, aerosol spray cans, and mercury thermometers
  • Hydrogen peroxide, lotions, and bulk liquid containers
Call Dispatch (309) 444-2313
