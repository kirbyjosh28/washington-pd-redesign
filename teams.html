Specialized Regional Divisions

Along with municipal 24/7 patrol, Washington Police officers serve on high-readiness regional tactical SWAT teams, digital forensics and cyber task forces, and trained auxiliary support units across Central Illinois.

CIERT SWAT: 6 Operators & Crisis Negotiators
Cyber Task Force: HSI & ICAC Digital Forensics
Auxiliary Cadre: 10 ILETSB-Certified Officers
TACTICAL SPECIAL WEAPONS CIERT SWAT

Central Illinois Emergency Response Team

Regional tactical response cadre: Six Washington police officers are cross-trained and actively assigned to CIERT as tactical operators and crisis negotiators for high-risk search warrants, barricaded subjects, hostage incidents, and critical regional callouts.

Tactical SWAT Operators Crisis Negotiation Team Multi-Agency Rapid Callout Armored Response Units
Multi-Jurisdictional Rapid Callout • 24/7 Readiness
DIGITAL FORENSICS HSI • ICAC

HSI Cyber & ICAC Task Forces

Inter-agency cyber investigations: Specialized detectives partner with Homeland Security Investigations and the Illinois Attorney General’s Internet Crimes Against Children (ICAC) Task Force on digital forensics, cyber exploitation, and technology crime cases.

Federal Task Force Officers Digital Forensics Lab Child Protection Shield Forensic Extraction Tools

SWORN SUPPORT CADRE AUXILIARY UNIT

Part-Time Police Unit

Sworn municipal reserve force: 10 state-certified sworn officers who augment full-time personnel, providing flexible operational staffing during summer festivals, Washington Square community events, high school athletics, and emergency traffic control.

COOPERATIVE REGIONAL JURISDICTIONS

Mutual Aid & Inter-Agency Alliances

The City of Washington maintains formal intergovernmental agreements and joint operational readiness pacts with municipal, county, state, and federal agencies.

STATEWIDE CRISIS MOBILIZATION

ILEAS Mutual Aid Compact

Member of the Illinois Law Enforcement Alarm System (ILEAS Region 6). Under this statutory mutual aid agreement, Washington Police can deploy or receive rapid-response sworn personnel, specialized vehicles, and disaster equipment during large-scale civil emergencies or natural disasters.

Region 6 Mutual Aid Tornado / Disaster Mobilization Rapid Deployment Force
CONSOLIDATED COMMUNICATIONS

TC3 Regional 911 Dispatch

Integrated with Tazewell County Consolidated Communications (TC3), linking Washington patrol units directly with Tazewell County Sheriff deputies, East Peoria, Morton, and Pekin police departments via common radio interoperability and live mobile CAD data networks.
